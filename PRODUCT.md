# PRODUCT.md — Hostinger Partner VIP Deals Platform

## 1. Product Identity & Register
- **Primary Surface (`/`)**: **Persuade Register (Decide & Act)** — Built for high-intent paid search visitors (Google Ads) and organic SEO visitors comparing Hostinger VPS (KVM 1/2/4/8), Managed WordPress/Web Hosting, Cloud Hosting, and AI/n8n hosting.
- **Secondary Surface (`/admin`)**: **Operate Register (Complete a Task)** — Built for the affiliate marketer to manage direct cart links per billing duration (`1m`, `12m`, `24m`, `48m`), monitor UTM/GCLID click analytics, and adjust countdown urgency without friction.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
- **Customer Benefit**: Instant **Extra 20% VIP Partner Discount** (`REFERRALCODE=EQOY9090OIJA`) stacked on top of Hostinger's seasonal discounts (up to 79% off).
- **Dual Conversion Path**:
  1. **Direct Cart Checkout**: One click loads the exact plan and selected billing duration (`1`, `12`, `24`, or `48` months) directly into Hostinger's checkout cart with the referral discount applied.
  2. **General Site Exploration**: One click opens Hostinger's official homepage with the 20% partner referral cookie activated so the visitor can browse any product freely.

## 3. Non-Negotiable Constraints
- Server-Side Rendered (SSR) HTML with JSON-LD (`Product`, `AggregateOffer`, `FAQPage`) for maximum Google Ads Quality Score and SEO speed.
- Full bilingual support (`ar` RTL and `en` LTR) and multi-currency conversion (`USD`, `AED`, `SAR`, `EGP`).
